
## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `beforeTitle` | `string` | — | Before Title configuration. |
| `afterTitle` | `string` | — | After Title configuration. |
| `before` (required) | `ReactNode` | — | Before configuration. |
| `after` (required) | `ReactNode` | — | After configuration. |
