## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data` | `number[]` | — | — |
| `width` | `number` | — | — |
| `height` | `number` | — | — |
| `color` | `string` | — | — |
| `filled` | `boolean` | `true` | Fill area under line. |
| `showEndDot` | `boolean` | `true` | Show a dot at the last data point. |
